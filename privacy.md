# Privacy Policy

_Thought Lab Studio · The Thought Lab TV · Last updated: 21 November 2026_

## What this is
Thought Lab Studio is a desktop application that runs on the computer of its owner, the creator of The Thought Lab TV. It is not a public service. It has no website, no server and no user accounts of its own.

## What it does with your TikTok account
When the owner connects their own TikTok account, the app asks TikTok for two permissions: to read the account's basic profile (display name) and to publish videos the owner chooses to upload. The app uses these only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own account.

## What data is stored, and where
- The access and refresh tokens that TikTok issues are stored in a private file on the owner's own computer.
- Nothing is sent to us or to any third party, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
- The app does not collect data about other people, viewers or followers.

## Retention and deletion
Tokens stay on the owner's computer until the owner disconnects the account or deletes the app's data folder. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and websites.

## Children
The app is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than its owner.

## Changes and contact
If this policy changes, this page is updated with a new date. Questions: send a message to the Instagram account @thethoughtlabtv or the YouTube channel @TheThoughtLabTV.
